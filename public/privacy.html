01 Summary

photodumps helps you review and clean your camera roll. We only collect what we need to run accounts, subscriptions, and optional cloud features you explicitly use.

02 Information we may collect

  • Account data: email, display name, and authentication identifiers from your sign-in provider.
  • Usage & diagnostics: anonymised or aggregated analytics and crash logs to fix bugs (you can limit tracking where your OS allows).
  • Purchase data: subscription status from Apple or Google — we do not receive your full payment card number.

03 Photos & media

Your gallery is accessed so you can swipe, archive, and delete. We do not upload your full photo library by default. Features that sync or backup data will call that out separately before you opt in.

On-device first. Core swipe and delete flows are designed to stay on your phone unless you choose a feature that needs cloud sync.

04 How we use information

To authenticate you, deliver Pro entitlements, improve stability, respond to support requests, and comply with applicable law.

05 Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data with processors who help us run the service (for example authentication, analytics, and payment validation) under contracts that require appropriate protection.

06 Retention

We keep account and billing records as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward for legal and accounting reasons.

07 Security

We use industry-standard practices, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

08 Your choices

You may request account deletion where applicable, adjust notification settings in the app, and manage subscriptions in your Apple ID or Google Play account settings.

For PDPA (Malaysia) requests, contact us at privacy@photodumps.app.

09 Children

photodumps is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region). Do not use the app if you are not old enough.

10 International users

If you use the app from outside Malaysia, your information may be processed in other countries where we or our vendors operate.
