Summary

photodumps helps you review and clean your camera roll. We only collect what we need for accounts, subscriptions, and features you choose to use.

Information we may collect

  • Account data — email, display name, and sign-in identifiers.
  • Usage & diagnostics — anonymised analytics and crash logs to fix bugs.
  • Purchase data — subscription status from Apple; we do not receive full card numbers.

Photos & media

Your gallery is accessed so you can swipe, archive, and delete. We do not upload your full photo library by default.

On-device first. Core swipe and delete flows stay on your phone unless you opt into a cloud feature.

How we use information

To authenticate you, deliver Pro entitlements, improve stability, respond to support, and comply with law.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We use processors (auth, analytics, payments) under contracts that require protection.

Retention

We keep account and billing records while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward.

Security
